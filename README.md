# Stealth Idler

**Stealth Idler** is a lightweight, high-performance Steam trading card farmer and hour booster built with **Rust** and **Tauri**. Designed as a purely **local-first desktop tool**, it runs entirely on your machine without cloud dependencies, external servers, or background telemetry. It skips the bloated Electron footprint of older alternatives, delivering near-zero CPU and RAM usage while staying completely invisible in the background.

---

### Key Features

* **100% Local & Private Execution**  
  Everything runs locally on your computer. Your credentials and session tokens never touch external servers or third-party APIs.

* **Safe & Seamless Authentication**  
  Sign in securely by scanning the native Steam Mobile QR code directly on your screen, or use traditional local credentials without exposing your Steam Guard details.

* **Multi-Account Ready**  
  Easily switch between and manage multiple Steam profiles from the dedicated sidebar without logging out or re-authenticating every time.

* **Smart Library & Drop Tracking**  
  Browse your entire game library with instant search and A–Z filters. The app automatically tracks card drop eligibility, pending drops, and total session runtime in real time.

* **Custom Boost Queue**  
  Add multiple games to your custom idle list, review your queue at a glance, and control your sessions with single-click **Start Boosting** and **Stop All** actions.

* **Resource-Efficient Rust Engine**  
  Native backend architecture emulates game presence without launching game executables, rendering 3D assets, or injecting code into system processes.
